## Decisions, experiments and AI collaboration

- The recorded combat direction is **player-driven MMA rhythm**: distance states, meaningful stamina, held guard with chip/break pressure, telegraph/feint/counter play, deterministic replay and one core shared by local and host execution.

- The animation rule remains evidence-led: approve the expressive parts that read correctly at game framing, keep failed clips uninstalled, and iterate on WOLF's retreat/guard treatment instead of lowering gates.
