## Projects touched

### Pitch Lane

Pitch Lane became a new public repository and shipped its first release in the same window.

- **The Logic-first singing coach reached M1–M4.** The JUCE 8 Audio Unit passes audio through unchanged, plots live vocal pitch against editable reference notes, analyzes a vocal stem into notes, imports/exports MIDI and restores its state with the Logic project. The implementation also includes tempo-map following, section markers, pitch smoothing and editing controls. [merged PR #1](https://github.com/Pak209/pitchlane/pull/1) · [core and tests](https://github.com/Pak209/pitchlane/commit/179beff50c28133283983b67aaecbe3d102e45dd) · [initial AU](https://github.com/Pak209/pitchlane/commit/23ed12ee77cbe999746a61af4de3c2f4d02c9c73).
- **Real-vocal and Logic usability problems were addressed before release.** Analyzer work corrected voicing and octave errors, marked likely harmony notes, supported lead-line selection, added take feedback, decoded common vocal formats, followed Logic tempo/signature changes, and fixed the “empty roll” experience by surfacing results and moving the view to the detected phrase. [analysis tuning](https://github.com/Pak209/pitchlane/commit/acd0fa2a481c3a8551a48a066998609498a67b0f) · [take feedback and harmony handling](https://github.com/Pak209/pitchlane/commit/71a67861e49cf00752334321242dfb41f7bf130f) · [visible Analyze Vocal result](https://github.com/Pak209/pitchlane/commit/edb998c83036ea35c4b3a7f2fdc79124078a7d9b).
- **Pitch Lane 0.1.0 shipped as a signed and Apple-notarized installer.** The release supplies a universal macOS package and checksum, supports Apple Silicon and Intel on macOS 11+, and documents the one-minute Logic installation and troubleshooting flow. [Pitch Lane 0.1.0](https://github.com/Pak209/pitchlane/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
