## Decisions and experiments

- **Relay authority stayed bounded.** Astra could continue only through newly approved outbox work; WAIT-FOR-PAK, merge, apply, settings, and credit decisions remained excluded.
- **Structure came before polish.** Three-distance readability, hero/support/filler classification, source hashes, anchor/seat validation, final-scene-only captures, and duplicate-pose refusal became explicit gates.
- **A suspected render-pipeline defect was falsified.** Placement and camera-seat selection—not lost procedural meshes—explained the bad evidence. The loop changed direction instead of preserving a convenient hypothesis.
- **Lighting targets were corrected.** Earlier brightness requirements proved too strict; Main-matched ambient, sun, skybox, and probes became the accepted baseline.
- **Routing stayed subordinate to gameplay.** Paths remain outside NPC battle zones, context is not moved merely to fit a path, a provisional 3.5 m capsule-center buffer applies, and several residential pads remain deferred.
- **Main application became a guarded decision.** Pak authorized per-district checkpoints, manifests, walks, before/after evidence, and rollback isolation. GitHub evidence does not independently prove NEXT61 executed.
