## Weekly outcome

This was HoloCity's densest integration week so far. The project moved from a connected desktop loop and early Arena work to an integrated interior, tournament presentation, host-backed account/economy features, a 13-Holobot fleet, and a signed build fifteen. HolobotsMobile absorbed the matching host authority and a large HoloCity-lite experience behind a default-off gate. Botcamp went from a new repository to a green iOS CI build and a focused interface cleanup.

The strongest accomplishment is not raw volume; it is convergence. Unity, Mobile, copy, host contracts and QA increasingly describe the same product. The largest remaining gap is acceptance: many systems are merged and well-instrumented, but physical-device, live-provider and hands-on play gates still trail the source.

## Important decisions and experiments

- Pak chose an **Arena-first story sequence** and turned the combat-feel profile on.
- Host authority remained the rule: Unity and Mobile present host replies rather than inventing health, rewards, onboarding state or tournament outcomes.
- HoloCity-lite may integrate into main only behind a **default-off flag**; TestFlight uses a separate gate-on release lane.
- Combat-space work moved to a **12 m town ring**, while evidence showed the old 60%-coverage target is not suitable for every venue. A venue-aware acceptance rule is still needed.
- The 40%-floor-plus-item ruling prevents healing a knocked-out Holobot into a worse state than the no-item fallback.
- The strafe/backstep pilot produced a valuable negative result: no owned Brawler or Synty clip passed on ACE or the 12 Mixar bodies. Purpose-built Blender animation is the recommended path.
- Replay infrastructure and deterministic twins advanced, but public issuance, live settlement and IL2CPP/device acceptance remain future gates.
