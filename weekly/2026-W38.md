# Week 38, 2026 — AI Build Review

**Window:** September 14–20, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)

## Executive summary

This was a concentrated **Holocity completion-and-proof week**. The dashboard moved from presentation surfaces to a controller-driven pause system; the city gained a residence ring, transit dressing, better lighting, LOD coverage, collision and material repairs; the day/night and screen-emission paths stopped corrupting shared assets; and the macOS profile established that the current standalone build can hold roughly 60 fps even while editor geometry and draw-call budgets remain far over target.

ACE, KUMA, Scrapling and resident work advanced through native captures, measured experiments and explicit rejection of failed hypotheses. The strongest pattern was not feature count but evidence discipline: physical-controller checks, red/green tests, repeatable captures, known-bad controls and commits that clearly distinguish a candidate from a shipped result.

These are repository-level outcomes, not a public-release claim. GitHub shows no release published during the week, and several controller, combat, character and performance gates remain open.
