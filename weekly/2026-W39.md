## Executive summary

This was the week Holocity’s separate systems began reading as a connected game slice: city routes became more legible, the Buddy flow moved into the world, encounters gained a shared presentation layer, HoloBall gained a continuous loop, and ACE’s approved moves reached game-facing wiring. The same evidence discipline also exposed a deeper problem: the roster’s skin weights—not only the animation keys—were blocking expressive poses.
