The result is meaningful forward motion without pretending that every merged branch is a finished build or release.

## Important decisions and experiments

- Keep new fight systems, readiness, real-time expeditions, and other contract changes behind explicit flags until native feel, scene binding, replay, or release evidence closes.
- Use hover-skating as HoloBall’s normal movement; reserve ACE’s aerial burst as its identity.
- Preserve the selected mech-armour ball, six-tab dashboard, and holographic Parts language.
- Never merge the Lite archive branch into `main`; build and archive from it separately.
- Rebuild districts one at a time in disposable sandboxes. No measured improvement or visual approval authorizes source-city application by itself.
- Keep Firestore rules strict. Repair legacy shapes only on the host when the transformation is deterministic; refuse malformed progression rather than invent values.
- Treat the flags-on recipe as per-function deployment state, not a universal fact, until older deployments are reconciled.
- Keep real-time expedition zone-run-6 default-off until zone-run-5 is reconciled and a clean Unity witness plus human tuning exists.
