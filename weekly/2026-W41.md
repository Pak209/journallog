## Executive summary

Week 41 moved HoloCity from a wide animation-and-combat polish push toward a clearer **PLAYABLE FIRST** path. Early in the week, Holocity strengthened combat feedback, fleet locomotion, progression, host integration, and reversible character-rig changes. The later work connected item inventory, health, HoloZone populations, town presence, Zell's story state, vendor presentation, and the first controlled Unity multiplayer runtime.

HolobotsMobile supplied the authority layer underneath that work through nine merged pull requests. LaunchAuthOS added collaboration infrastructure and safeguards for customer data, staging, ownership, deletion, and payment recovery.

This was substantial repository-level progress, not a public release. Multiplayer remains flag-off; two Mobile PRs remain open; native test baselines retain known failures; the second Mac multiplayer build and real two-client witness were not complete; and deployment records were not independently confirmed.
